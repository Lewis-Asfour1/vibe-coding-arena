How it works

Three steps. No accounts.

01

Grant a single permission

Focus Guard uses the OS accessibility layer to see which surface you're on — never what you type, view, or send.

02

Detect short-form only

Vertical, auto-advancing video feeds are fingerprinted. Reels, Shorts, TikTok. Your feed, DMs and search stay free.

03

Interrupt, then redirect

At your limit, a calm full-screen guard appears — and The Compass helps you decide what to do with your reclaimed time.

Why it's different
