How it works

Three steps. No accounts.

01

Grant access

Focus Guard detects when you are in short-form video mode.

02

The timer starts

After 7 seconds (demo), a calm warning appears to interrupt the loop.

03

Redirect & talk

Switch to DMs, Home, or talk to The Compass. Your data never leaves.

Privacy architecture

Local-only by design.

No servers. No logins. No network requests. Everything is computed on your device.
